# Artwork sources

The paintings used by Dark Ages are in the public domain. The packaged files
retain their original color and tonality; no black-and-white conversion,
contrast treatment, denoising, sharpening, or AI editing was applied.

1. **Johan Christian Dahl — Larvik by Moonlight** — [National Museum of Norway](https://www.nasjonalmuseet.no/en/collection/object/NG.M.00034) · [public-domain source file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Johan_Christian_Dahl_-_Larvik_by_Moonlight_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg)
2. **Johan Christian Dahl — Copenhagen Harbor by Moonlight** — [Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/439343), Open Access/Public Domain.
3. **Aert van der Neer — River View by Moonlight** — [Rijksmuseum](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/collection/object/Riviergezicht-bij-maanlicht--dba7201e0bdbd418d44a1acf79ac1be1) · [public-domain source file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Riviergezicht_bij_maanlicht_Rijksmuseum_SK-A-3245.jpeg)
4. **Peder Balke — The North Cape by Moonlight** — [Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/441379), Open Access/Public Domain.
5. **Pieter Claesz — Still Life with a Skull and a Writing Quill** — [Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/435904), Open Access/Public Domain.
