  `activateAtCursor`), retries once after the switcher unmaps, and sets
  `lastError = "Focus failed"` as a string. Address-form `hl.focus` remains
  the inner fallback when the object is missing.
- `default/ultimate/chrome-tokens.json` is consumed by hyprbars (`bar_color` /
  `col.text`) and Superbar glass (`Taskbar.qml` FileView). Caption button
  colors stay private. Light theme still does not propagate.

  Do not restart SDDM from those scripts.
